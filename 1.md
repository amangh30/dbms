**3NF (Third Normal Form)** in short:  

A table is in **3NF** if:  

1. It is already in **2NF** (no partial dependency on a composite key), and  
2. **No non-prime attribute** is transitively dependent on the primary key.  

*Transitively dependent* means:  
If `A → B` and `B → C`, then non-prime attribute `C` should not depend on `B` (which is not a candidate key). In other words, *all non-key attributes must depend directly on the key*, not on another non-key column.
